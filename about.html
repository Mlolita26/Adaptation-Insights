Why this exists

Projects learn a lot about what works in adaptation, but it stays in reports few people read. Adaptation Insights puts that evidence in one place you can search and compare.

What the data covers

Climate adaptation projects in African agriculture and food systems, as reported in their completion reports and evaluations. For each project the data gives where it worked, who funded and implemented it, whom it targeted, why it was needed, and the results it reported, each with the page it came from.

The pilot covers eight projects in eight countries, read from nine documents. The full dataset will cover the documents found in scope when the funders' repositories were screened in November 2026: 225 of 1,647.

Region
Africa; regional projects count when African countries take part
