Read with care

  • Figures are as reported by the projects, not independently verified.
  • Some places are not geocoded yet and are shown at an approximate position.
  • The pilot records have not yet had a human QA review.
  • Mid-term documents give values part-way through a project, not final ones.
  • Each project counts its beneficiaries in its own way. Figures from different projects are not always comparable.
  • Eight projects are a pilot, not a sample. Totals across them do not describe adaptation in Africa.
